REALITY

Privacy

Version 1.0 · effective 10 November 2026

Reality is a game. It stores the least it needs to keep your citizen, show your buildings on the shared map and stop abuse. It has no advertising and no third-party analytics, and nothing about you is sold.

Who else handles it

  • Vercel hosts the game and file storage; Neon hosts the database.
  • OpenFreeMap serves map tiles and Overpass (OpenStreetMap) serves street data. They receive the map area you look at, like any map site.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Google, Telegram and OpenAI are involved only if you use Google sign-in, open the game in Telegram, or create an avatar.

What other players can see

Your citizen name, your buildings and their rounded positions, and your place on the leaderboard. Never your email, your Telegram or Google identity, or a precise home location.

Money

All money in Reality is play money. There are no purchases, deposits, withdrawals or payouts, so no payment details are collected.

Keeping and deleting

  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the local save and signs you out.
  • To delete your citizen and everything linked to it on our server, or to get a copy of it, open a request at github.com/DansiDanutz/Reality/issues without posting private details; we will arrange verification with you.

Children

Reality is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this notice changes, the version and date at the top change with it, and the history is public in the source repository.

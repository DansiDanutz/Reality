# Reality full audit — 2026-07-15

## Executive verdict

Reality is a strong, unusually well-tested open beta with a coherent product vision, a substantial real-time simulation, fail-closed real-settlement gates, and 1,510 passing unit/integration tests. It is **not yet ready for a trusted shared economy or real-value launch**.

The blocking problem is not a missing feature. It is authority: the client store, Neon intent ledger, and Blob Founder Area each own overlapping money, health, time, assets, and transactions. Until there is one atomic server authority, users can mint local state, concurrent requests can lose writes, and normal actions can postpone the simulation clock.

**Release recommendation: REQUEST CHANGES.** Continue open-beta gameplay, but keep payouts, deposits, withdrawals, land sales, death/inheritance, and TON settlement disabled.

## Confirmed findings

### P0 — must resolve before trusted economy

#### P0.1 — No single authoritative economy or simulation

The client mutates and persists local money/body state in `src/store/gameStore.ts:1378-1422` and `src/store/gameStore.ts:3674-3691`. `/api/intent` owns a separate Neon ledger (`api/intent.ts:135-147`, `api/intent.ts:194-206`) but has no production gameplay caller. Founder Area maintains another Blob balance and simulator, and `FounderAreaPanel` builds an in-memory world-sim projection (`src/components/panels/FounderAreaPanel.tsx:1001-1016`).

Impact: local edits can mint game state; formulas and clocks diverge; no component can prove canonical money, ownership, or health.

Action: choose Neon ledger plus canonical `worldSim` as authority, wire all value-changing actions through it, migrate Founder Area, and demote local storage to cache/projection.

#### P0.2 — Founder Area writes are last-write-wins

`api/reality-area.ts:2083-2090` reads a Blob snapshot and `api/reality-area.ts:4308-4319` overwrites it with `allowOverwrite: true` without revision/CAS. Concurrent intents can validate the same funds/capacity and both return success while one update is lost.

PR #1020 now makes sequential same-hour capacity accounting honest, but it cannot solve concurrent same-snapshot requests.

Action: use the existing revision-aware repository and advance/apply/save flow in `src/game/worldSimServer.ts:1455-1483`; persist in Postgres under a per-area transaction/lock with idempotency keys and unique ledger IDs.

#### P0.3 — User actions can postpone simulation indefinitely

`api/reality-area.ts:5641-5647` treats `updatedAt` as the simulation cursor, while build, hire, service, and insurance intents also overwrite it (`api/reality-area.ts:5512`, `5558`, `5703`, `5766`). Any action less than one hour apart can prevent needs, wages, insurance, and hospitalization from advancing.

Action: add `simulatedThroughAt`, keep mutation metadata separate, and always advance to command time before applying an intent.

#### P0.4 — First Neon ledger action accepts a client-chosen seed

`api/intent.ts:194-205` accepts and clamps `predictedBalance`, then uses it as `p_seed`. The day-zero score ceiling reaches $300,000 (`api/_scoresPg.ts:23-29`), while the intended regular-citizen starter credit is $2,500.

Action: create a server-owned genesis ledger row during registration from canonical founder status; remove client seed authority and retain predicted balance only for drift reporting.

### P1 — high priority integrity and security

#### P1.1 — Shared-world placement proves identity, not ownership

`api/world.ts:74-111` authenticates a citizen and validates shape/coordinates, but does not prove catalog item, purchase, inventory ownership, territorial reach, or placement intent.

Action: atomically consume server-owned placeable inventory and validate catalog kind/reach as the committed result of a server intent.

#### P1.2 — Citizen credentials are durable localStorage bearer tokens

Registration returns a UUID bearer token (`api/register.ts:119-177`); the database has no session expiry/revocation columns (`scripts/db/schema.sql:10-18`); persisted Zustand state retains the token (`src/store/gameStore.ts:3657-3691`).

Action: introduce expiring/revocable sessions and rotation, preferably Secure/HttpOnly/SameSite cookies with CSRF protection or short-lived access plus protected refresh credentials.

#### P1.3 — Health laws differ across three simulation paths

The plan and main engine use dehydration −6/h, starvation/exhaustion −3/h, and healthy recovery +2/h (`docs/plan/02-HEALTH-SYSTEM.md:80-89`, `src/game/engine.ts:170-183`). `worldSim` uses different penalties/regeneration (`src/game/worldSim.ts:3263-3278`), and Founder Area applies 15 HP per critical need with different hospitalization behavior (`api/reality-area.ts:6168-6179`).

Action: export one approved health policy and add cross-adapter golden tests.

#### P1.4 — Multi-day catch-up silently stops after 24 hours

`api/reality-area.ts:5641-5647` caps a request at 24 ticks; GET performs one pass and returns (`api/reality-area.ts:5018-5035`).

Action: use analytical spans or bounded batches until target time is reached; if continuation is required, report it explicitly.

#### P1.5 — Persisted Founder Area validation is shallow

`api/reality-area.ts:6787-6800` does not validate citizens, timestamps, nested businesses, needs, or transactions before normalization calls `.map()` (`api/reality-area.ts:2140-2155`).

Action: reuse the complete `worldSimCodec` decoder and quarantine/reject invalid snapshots.

#### P1.6 — Expensive Overpass work is public and unauthenticated

`api/overpass.ts:28-59` permits cache-bypass coordinates and up to three upstream calls totaling 55 seconds; `vercel.json:4-7` allows a 60-second function. There is no per-IP quota or global circuit breaker.

Action: add edge/IP rate limiting, coordinate bucketing/admission, a global breaker, tighter budgets, and optionally citizen authentication.

#### P1.7 — Map interaction is hidden from assistive technology

`src/components/map/WorldMap.tsx:747` marks the MapLibre container `aria-hidden`, including interactive marker buttons. Owned holdings are clickable `div` elements (`WorldMap.tsx:363-379`) and inspect behavior is pointer/touch-only (`src/components/map/mapQuickInfo.ts:111-131`).

Action: hide only decorative canvas, use semantic buttons, and provide a keyboard/screen-reader marker list or equivalent accessible interaction path.

#### P1.8 — The planned first-week path is not E2E/CI-gated

The plan requires job, needs, study, gather, house, worker, and business lifecycle coverage (`docs/plan/11-DAY-LOOP-IMPLEMENTATION.md:114-118`). `e2e/first-session.spec.ts` covers only citizen creation, food, and work. CI runs Vitest/build only (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:17-21`) and Playwright has Desktop Chrome only.

Action: add deterministic first-week and 320px mobile smoke; gate Chromium in CI and schedule broader browsers if latency is high.

### P2 — should resolve before scale

- **Leaderboard trusts client name/net worth:** `api/leaderboard.ts:54-73`; derive both from canonical citizen/ledger data.
- **Insurance renewal capacity diverges:** Blob renewals ignore capacity (`api/reality-area.ts:5911-5945`) while `worldSim` reserves it.
- **Transaction IDs can collide:** `api/reality-area.ts:5670-5678` uses millisecond/intent/business without uniqueness enforcement.
- **Avatar quota reserves after the billed call:** `api/avatar.ts:180-220`; reserve atomically before generation.
- **Registration/world quotas are check-then-write:** `api/register.ts:91-117`, `api/world.ts:96-111`, `api/_worldPg.ts:74-90`.
- **Telegram identity is not unique:** `telegram_user_id` is nullable/non-unique and registration can bind multiple citizens during init-data validity.
- **Blocked actions are not consistently self-explaining:** Work, construction, and business controls disable without always naming exact missing resources/budget/labor.
- **Mobile sheet semantics conflict:** `MobileHud` declares a non-modal sheet, while `useFocusTrap` prevents keyboard users reaching bottom navigation.
- **Snapshots grow without bound:** transactions/events/reviews append forever; every request downloads the full Blob.
- **Dashboard work becomes O(businesses × transactions):** each business scans the full ledger.
- **Server clock scans Blob areas serially:** the cron path awaits each area one at a time.
- **Mobile bundles are heavy:** build emitted roughly 841 kB main JS, 1,056 kB WorldMap, and 587 kB streetScene before gzip.
- **Dev/build dependency advisories:** ten advisories exist below `@vercel/node`; production dependency audit is clean.
- **Browser security headers are not repository-defined:** no tested CSP/frame/referrer/permissions policy is present.
- **README is stale:** it still describes a client-only app/no backend and an outdated map stack.

### Local workspace hygiene

- The Git remote contained an embedded GitHub credential. The remote is now token-free and GitHub CLI authentication is used for API operations. The exposed credential must be rotated.
- Ignored Gemini environment files are duplicated and mode `0644`; restrict to `0600`, rotate if exposure is plausible, and remove obsolete backups only through approved secret hygiene.

## Strengths to preserve

- Real payouts, withdrawals, deposits, TON settlement, death, inheritance, and land sale are fail-closed.
- SQL reviewed is parameterized; no confirmed SQL injection was found.
- Intent/API field allowlists prevent server-owned-field smuggling.
- Telegram HMAC/freshness/bot validation and operator token scope/expiry checks are strong.
- Citizen bearer values are randomly generated and only hashes are stored server-side.
- The Neon ledger append serializes per citizen and calculates prices/wages server-side.
- `worldSimServer` already supplies advance-before-intent, revision-aware save, write conflicts, and useful tests.
- The mobile shell has labeled navigation, adequate targets, reduced-motion handling, focus restoration, and no 320px overflow in the smoke check.
- The product plan has clear design laws and measurable D7/contribution success targets.

## Ordered execution plan

The implementation sequence is tracked in `.omx/plans/autopilot-impl.md`. In short:

1. Server-owned ledger genesis and Telegram uniqueness.
2. Atomic revision-safe Founder Area repository and idempotency.
3. Separate simulation cursor and complete catch-up.
4. One health/body policy and one canonical simulation.
5. Authoritative placement, inventory, and leaderboard projections.
6. Expiring sessions, atomic quotas, and browser security policy.
7. Accessible map/actions plus CI-gated first-week/mobile E2E.
8. Snapshot/index/bundle optimization and final launch evidence.

## Completion definition

Reality becomes eligible for a trusted-economy review only when concurrent intent tests, ledger conservation, authoritative ownership, session revocation, complete real-time catch-up, accessible core journeys, and CI-gated first-week E2E all pass. Real settlement remains a separate compliance and custody decision after that engineering gate.

# 08 — Content Roadmap

*From 78 items to a civilization's worth of stuff — designed so 90% of it can be built
by contributors as data PRs (06), with invariant tests as the guardrails.*

---

## The catalog engine (how content scales safely)

Every item is data in `catalog.ts` guarded by tests: unique ids, payback bands for
businesses, affordability rules, "every item does something," never-pay-to-win lint.
A contributor (or their Claude) can add fifty items without touching logic — CI proves
they didn't break the economy. Images: the market art pipeline (dark-studio product
shots, night storefronts) is documented; generation is one prompt per item.

## Wave plan

**Wave 1 — Depth of daily life (78 → ~200 items)**
Groceries as ingredients (cook combos), regional foods (mici for Romania — city-aware
catalog entries!), wardrobe tiers, home furniture with room slots, pets (companionship
= fun regen aura; a real responsibility: they eat), bicycles-to-EV ladder gaps.

**Wave 2 — Careers (5 → 20)**
Nurse, teacher, chef, taxi/rideshare, electrician, architect, pharmacist, journalist,
programmer specializations. Each career: wage curve, level gate, education path, and a
*workplace verb* (what your shift narrates). Gigs (30-min micro-shifts) for mobile
sessions.

**Wave 3 — Education as a tree**
From one-off XP purchases to certification trees: high-school equivalence → trade
certs → degrees → licenses (pharmacy license gates pharmacy ownership). Night classes
(2h real) vs full-time. Scholarships from city treasuries — policy meets content.

**Wave 4 — Events & seasons (the retention engine)**
Weekly city events (markets, concerts — fun economy), seasonal reality (weather by
real season per hemisphere; winter heating costs!), world events (harvest, elections),
and **real-calendar holidays localized**: Christmas lights in December Street Mode,
Ramadan night markets, Lunar New Year. Rule #1 applied to culture.

**Wave 5 — Businesses to 100 types**
Tiered by capital: street (cart, kiosk, stall) → local (bakery, barber, café, pharmacy,
gym) → institutional (clinic, school, hotel, factory) → empire (utility, airline,
bank — banks lend citizen-to-citizen at interest, on-ledger). Each type: real startup
cost research, staffing needs (Phase 2 jobs), and a Street Mode storefront skin.

## Content laws

Every item: real price research (cite in PR), one clear job in the loops (need, perk,
income, or status), a persona from 04 it serves. No lootboxes, no gacha, no timers
that beg — Rule #1 already owns time.
